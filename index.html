FAQ

How much does a typical run cost?

You pay per result. The real runs above cost $0.024 (200 reviews), $0.075 (50 pages) and $0.03 (one rank check). Apify gives new accounts free monthly credit to try them.

Why use the website endpoint for App Store reviews?

Apple's customer-reviews RSS feed comes back empty for most apps in 2026. The App Store website reads reviews from its own endpoint, 20 per page, newest first, and that is what the reviews tool uses, with paced requests.

What happens if a page or app fails?

Failed items are not charged, and the results say why reading stopped.

Where is the source and the MCP listing?

The MCP server listings are on GitHub: ByteBlisk/mcp-servers.
